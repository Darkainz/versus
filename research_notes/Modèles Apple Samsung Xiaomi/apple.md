## Verification limits

This is a verified subset of 22 distinct models, not a claim that Apple has only 22 eligible products. I did not reach 30: Apple Support technical-spec requests were rate-limited during this pass, while several discontinued product URLs redirected to current lineup pages. To avoid unsupported product names, dates, or features, I left those candidates out rather than fill gaps from memory or non-Apple sources. Products already listed in the catalog were excluded, including iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 17, iPhone 17e, AirPods Max 2, AirPods Pro 3, AirPods 5, Apple Watch Ultra 4, Apple Watch Series 12, and Apple Watch SE 3.
