## Exclusions and evidence limits

- The catalog already includes Galaxy S26, Galaxy S26 Ultra, Galaxy Z Fold8, Galaxy Z Flip8, Galaxy A57 5G, Galaxy Buds4 and Galaxy Buds4 Pro, Galaxy Watch9, Galaxy Watch Ultra2, and Galaxy Fit3. Those names are therefore omitted; storage and color options were not counted as separate products.
- I stopped at 21 rather than force 30. A number of older Samsung product URLs now redirect to a broad family page or show only an out-of-stock notice (for example Galaxy S24/S24 Ultra, Galaxy Z Fold6 and several older Buds/Watch pages). Those pages did not provide enough model-specific official feature detail for additional candidates under the requested evidence standard.
- Product pages were checked directly. For several pages Samsung has updated content for newer software, promotions or a same-name refresh; the listed launch year is the approximate original model-generation year, not a color/storage release date.
